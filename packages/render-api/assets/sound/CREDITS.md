## Music

Excerpts of human-made tracks, one per film's need: each cut around the downbeat the film's turn lands on, tempo-locked onto the film's 120 BPM grid (or 60 or 80) with Rubber Band where it was not already on it, peak-normalised to −1 dBFS, faded at both ends and stored as 48 kHz stereo Opus at 160 kbit/s. `library.json` lists the same tracks with their measured traits. Licence read on each track's own page (2026-11-12 for the two tracks kept from round 1, 2026-11-13 for the rest): each Free Music Archive page links only `creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/`; each OpenGameArt page's licence block lists only CC0. A track with no steady pulse, or one more than 6% from a 60, 80 or 120 BPM grid, is not stretched ("no lock"): only its drop is placed on the film's turn. Blueprint and glyph excerpts hold 11 s before the drop, for their late turns. `Epic Boss Battle` is posted on OpenGameArt by SubspaceAudio, the account of its composer Juhani Junkala.
