# Three looks proofs

Glasgow rev C3 comes from the Glasgow project under 0BSD. The three proofs use silk reel renderer output from that board.

The stills share a projected proof surface. Glyph uses the actual glyph marks on a solid black surface. No decorative mounting holes or concentric border circles are added.

The image generator is `.context/sections/cards.py`. Its projection coordinates are in `.context/sections/card-quad.json`. Each proof has 1× and 2× WebP versions.
